Liar Game begins with 5 players and exactly one Liar. There will be 1 clue round(s), followed by one private vote from each player for another player. If the group accuses a non-liar, the Liar wins. If it accuses the Liar, the Liar gets one final word guess and still wins if correct; otherwise the non-liars win. Non-liars must both identify the Liar and keep the word difficult to guess. All clues are public. Malformed or self-votes abstain; ties accuse the earliest tied player in the configured player order.
